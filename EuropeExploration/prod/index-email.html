Stephanie LeBlanc

What to Know About Brussels, Belgium

Brussels in late summer is bustling with outdoor activities and events. Expect a mix of historical attractions and vibrant local culture during your stay. Markets and cafes are ideal for budget-friendly dining.

Local customs: Brussels locals appreciate politeness; a simple 'bonjour' or 'merci' goes a long way. Tipping is not obligatory but rounding up the bill is appreciated.

Best times of day: Mornings are great for visiting popular attractions before crowds arrive. Late afternoons are ideal for enjoying coffee at outdoor cafes.

Transportation quirks: Brussels has an extensive public transport system, including trams and metro. Many attractions are within walking distance in the city center.

Safety: Brussels is generally safe, but remain vigilant in crowded tourist areas against pickpockets. Keep bags secure and avoid displaying valuables.

Crowd patterns: Expect larger crowds at major attractions during weekends, especially in the late afternoon. Weekdays may be quieter for sightseeing.

Local etiquette: Politeness in interactions is key; always greet shopkeepers or waitstaff. Eating or drinking in public transportation is frowned upon.

1-2 hours 🗺️

It's especially popular for its photo opportunities.📌 Tickets can be purchased on-site; consider buying in advance to skip the queue.

★ 4.6/51-2 hours 🗺️

1 hour 🗺️

🏛️Manneken Pis🔗
★ 4.5/530 min 🗺️
