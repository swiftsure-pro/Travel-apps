Brussels, Belgium

Stephanie LeBlanc

What to Know About Brussels, Belgium

Brussels in late summer offers mild weather and fewer tourists than peak season. This is a great time to explore the city's rich history and culture without the crowds. Look out for local markets and street food options.

Local customs: Belgians appreciate politeness; a simple 'bonjour' or 'merci' goes a long way. Tipping is not mandatory but rounding up the bill is appreciated.

Best times of day: Mornings are ideal for visiting popular attractions like Grand Place before the crowds arrive. Late afternoons are perfect for enjoying local cafes.

Transportation quirks: Public transport is efficient, with trams, buses, and metro services available. Consider purchasing a day pass for unlimited travel.

Safety: Brussels is generally safe, but be aware of pickpockets in crowded areas. Keep belongings secure and be cautious in busy spots.

Crowd patterns: Expect more tourists in the afternoons at major attractions. Early mornings and late evenings are quieter times for exploration.

Local etiquette: Respect quiet areas and avoid loud conversations in public transport. It's customary to greet shopkeepers when entering and leaving.

What to Know About Berlin, Germany

Berlin in September provides a mix of culture and history in a mild autumn climate. It's an ideal time to explore the city's outdoor attractions without the summer crowds. Expect local markets and street food options throughout the city.

Local customs: Germans appreciate punctuality, so arrive on time for any scheduled activities or reservations.

Best times of day: Mornings are quieter for sightseeing, while evenings are lively with locals dining out and enjoying nightlife.

Transportation quirks: Public transport is efficient, but be prepared to validate your ticket before boarding. Bicycles are common, and bike rentals are widely available.

Safety: Berlin is generally safe for travelers; however, be aware of your surroundings, especially in busy tourist areas.

Crowd patterns: Expect larger crowds at popular sites on weekends, especially in the late morning to early afternoon.

Local etiquette: It's common to greet shopkeepers and waitstaff when entering and leaving establishments. Tipping around 10-15% is appreciated.
