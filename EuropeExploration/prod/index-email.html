What to Know About Brussels, Belgium

Brussels in late summer offers a mix of cultural attractions and outdoor experiences. Expect warm weather, vibrant street life, and access to many cafes and markets for low-cost dining.

Local customs: It's customary to greet locals with a friendly 'Bonjour' or 'Hallo' depending on the language spoken in the area.

Best times of day: Mornings are great for visiting popular attractions to avoid crowds, while evenings offer a lively atmosphere in cafes and markets.

Transportation quirks: Public transportation is efficient, with trams and buses running frequently. A single ticket can be used for multiple modes of transport.

Safety: Brussels is generally safe, but be cautious of pickpockets in crowded areas and on public transport.

Crowd patterns: Expect higher foot traffic around major attractions during the day, particularly on weekends. Early mornings are quieter.

Local etiquette: Dining typically takes place later in the evening, around 8 PM. Tipping is appreciated but not mandatory.

What to Know About Amsterdam, Netherlands

Amsterdam in early September offers a mix of comfortable weather and fewer tourists. Expect a variety of local dining options, including street food and cafes, fitting for a budget-conscious traveler.

Local customs: Bicycles are a primary mode of transport; be mindful of cyclists when walking. It's common to greet shopkeepers when entering and leaving stores.

Best times of day: Mornings are often quieter at major attractions, making it an ideal time for visits. Evening strolls along the canals are particularly pleasant as the city lights reflect on the water.

Transportation quirks: Public transport includes trams, buses, and ferries. Purchase an OV-chipkaart for convenient travel on all forms of public transport.

Safety: Amsterdam is generally safe, but be cautious of pickpockets in crowded areas and on public transport. Stay alert, especially in tourist hotspots.

Crowd patterns: September sees a decrease in tourist traffic compared to summer, resulting in a more relaxed atmosphere. Popular attractions may still get busy on weekends.

Local etiquette: When dining, it’s polite to wait for the host to seat you. Tipping is appreciated but not mandatory; rounding up the bill is common.
