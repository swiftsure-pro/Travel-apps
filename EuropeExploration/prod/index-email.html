Stephanie LeBlanc

What to Know About Brussels, Belgium

Brussels in late summer offers mild weather and fewer crowds. Enjoy local cafes and markets for budget-friendly dining. The city is easy to navigate on foot or by public transport.

Local customs: It is customary to greet with a handshake and say 'bonjour' or 'bonsoir' depending on the time of day.

Best times of day: Mornings are ideal for exploring popular attractions before crowds build.

Transportation quirks: Public transport is efficient, with trams and buses running frequently. Consider getting a day pass for unlimited travel.

Safety: Be aware of pickpockets in crowded areas. Keep your belongings secure and avoid displaying valuables.

Crowd patterns: Expect larger crowds in popular areas during the afternoon. Early mornings and late evenings are quieter.

Local etiquette: When dining, it is polite to wait for everyone at the table to be served before starting your meal.

🏛️Grand Place🔗
★ 4.7/51 hr 🗺️

🏛️Sablon District🔗
★ 4.6/51–2 hrs 🗺️

🏛️Manneken Pis🔗
★ 4.5/530 min 🗺️
This small bronze statue of a boy urinating is a quirky symbol of Brussels. It's often dressed in various costumes and attracts many visitors.📌 Best visited early in the morning to avoid crowds.
🏛️Atomium🔗
1–2 hrs 🗺️
An building and museum, the Atomium represents an iron crystal magnified 165 billion times. Visitors can explore the spheres and enjoy panoramic views of the city from the top.📌 Tickets are required for entry.

What to Know About Prague, Czech Republic

Prague in September features mild weather, making it ideal for exploring the city's rich history and architecture. Be prepared for some crowds at popular attractions, but overall the city is less busy than during peak summer months. Local markets and street food vendors provide budget-friendly dining options.

Local customs: Tipping is customary, around 10% of the bill in restaurants. Greeting people with a handshake is common.

Best times of day: Early mornings are best for visiting major attractions to avoid crowds. Late afternoons provide beautiful lighting for photography.

Transportation quirks: Public transport is efficient and affordable; consider purchasing a travel pass for convenience. Trams and metro are popular ways to get around.

Safety: Prague is generally safe, but be cautious of pickpockets in crowded areas. Keep valuables secure and be aware of your surroundings.

Crowd patterns: Crowds peak around noon at major tourist sites, especially Old Town Square and Prague Castle. Visiting early or late in the day is advisable.

Local etiquette: Respect local customs, such as not speaking loudly in public places. It's polite to say 'Dobrý den' (good day) when entering shops or restaurants.
