What to Know About Callahan's Lodge, Oregon

Callahan's Lodge functions as the main resupply point for PCT hikers in this section of southern Oregon. June conditions feature dry trails and warm afternoons with cool nights. The lodge accepts mail drops and offers limited hiker services.

Local customs: Hikers typically arrive mid-afternoon and depart early the next morning. Quiet hours begin at 10pm.

Best times of day: Early morning offers the coolest temperatures for continued hiking. Evenings provide the best chance to meet other northbound travelers at the lodge.

Transportation quirks: No vehicle access to the lodge itself. Hikers reach it solely on foot via the PCT.

Safety: Carry sufficient water between sources. Afternoon thunderstorms remain possible in June.

Crowd patterns: Light foot traffic on weekdays. Weekends see more day hikers near the lodge trailhead.

Local etiquette: Sign the hiker register at the lodge. Pack out all trash from trail segments.

What to Know About Mazama Village, Oregon

Mazama Village sits inside Crater Lake National Park at 6,000 ft. July days are dry with cool nights; carry layers and water. The two-day stay allows one full loop of Rim Drive plus two hikes.

Local customs: Ranger programs begin at dusk; arrive early for seating. Quiet hours start at 10pm in the campground.

Best times of day: Morning light on the east rim is clearest before 10am. Afternoon winds pick up on exposed viewpoints after 2pm.

Transportation quirks: No public transit inside the park. The Rim Drive is one-way in sections; follow posted direction.

Safety: Snow patches can linger on north slopes into July. Carry bear spray and filter all water sources.

Crowd patterns: Parking lots at Watchman and Garfield fill by 11am on weekends. Mid-week mornings are quieter.

Local etiquette: Stay on paved paths at viewpoints to protect fragile pumice soils. Pack out all trash; no bins on trails.
