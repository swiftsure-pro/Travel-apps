What to Know About Callahan's Lodge, Oregon

Callahan's Lodge sits directly on the PCT at the Siskiyou crest. June 27 falls in peak thru-hiker season with steady foot traffic. The lodge provides the only reliable services for many trail miles in either direction.

Local customs: Thru-hikers often resupply and rest here; yield to them on narrow trail sections.

Best times of day: Early morning offers the coolest temperatures for any additional hiking. Evenings cool quickly once the sun drops behind the ridge.

Transportation quirks: No public transit reaches the lodge; all access is on foot or by prior arrangement with the lodge.

Safety: Carry adequate water between sources; afternoon thunderstorms can develop in June.

Crowd patterns: Midday sees the highest number of arriving hikers; mornings and late afternoons are quieter around the lodge.

Local etiquette: Pack out all trash; the lodge maintains bear-proof storage for food.

What to Know About Mazama Village, Oregon

Mazama Village serves as the main campground and service hub inside Crater Lake National Park. July is peak season with full services but high visitation. Two days allows time for rim hikes and one full circuit of Rim Drive.

Local customs: Park entrance fee is collected at the south entrance station; keep the receipt for the duration of the stay.

Best times of day: Early morning offers the clearest lake views and cooler temperatures for hiking; afternoon thunderstorms are common in July.

Transportation quirks: The park shuttle runs from Mazama Village to Rim Village every 30 minutes; private vehicles are allowed on Rim Drive but parking is limited at popular trailheads.

Safety: Carry at least two liters of water per person on any rim hike; elevation exceeds 7,000 ft and sun exposure is intense.

Crowd patterns: Trailheads and parking areas fill by 10am on weekends; mid-week mornings are noticeably quieter.

Local etiquette: Stay on established trails to protect fragile pumice soils; do not approach wildlife including deer and marmots.

Shelter Cove Resort sits on the PCT route near the southern Oregon coast and serves as a resupply and rest point for through-hikers. July weather is mild with morning fog common. The stop is a single-night pause between Mazama Village and Elk Lake Resort.

Local customs: PCT hikers often leave small caches of supplies at the resort office; confirm space in advance.

Best times of day: Morning arrival on trail avoids afternoon coastal wind. Evening light improves ocean views from nearby bluffs.

Transportation quirks: No vehicle access from the PCT; the resort provides limited shuttle service to nearby towns on request.

Safety: Carry a water filter for trail sources and watch for slippery rocks on coastal paths after fog.

Crowd patterns: Through-hiker traffic peaks in July but the resort remains quiet compared with highway stops.

Local etiquette: Sign the PCT register at the office and pack out all trash from beach areas.
