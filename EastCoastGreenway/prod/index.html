What to Know About Kennebunk, Maine

Kennebunk serves as a single-night stop on the East Coast Greenway route. Early September offers mild temperatures and reduced beach crowds after the holiday weekend.

Local customs: Many restaurants close early on weeknights; check hours before arrival.

Best times of day: Morning light favors beach walks before parking fills. Evening offers quieter riverfront paths.

Transportation quirks: Bike racks are available at Dock Square and beach lots; Main Street has narrow shoulders outside dedicated paths.

Safety: Watch for deer on rural road segments at dusk and carry lights for any evening riding.

Crowd patterns: Tourist numbers drop sharply after Labor Day with easier access to restaurants and parking.

Local etiquette: Yield to pedestrians on shared riverfront paths and keep voices low near residential areas.

Portsmouth functions as a compact coastal stop on the East Coast Greenway with bike-friendly streets and waterfront paths. September weather supports comfortable riding with cooler evenings. Most sites lie within a one-mile radius of Market Square.

What to Know About Newburyport, Massachusetts

Newburyport sits at the mouth of the Merrimack River with direct bike access from Portsmouth via coastal paths. September weather supports two full days of outdoor time with cooler mornings. The town center and refuge are compact enough to cover on foot or bike after arrival.

Local customs: Many waterfront restaurants open early for breakfast and close by 9 pm. Refill water at public fountains along the boardwalk.

Best times of day: Morning light on the estuary draws birds at the refuge. Evenings bring quieter boardwalk walks after day visitors depart.

Transportation quirks: Bike lanes on main roads through town are marked but narrow in places. No public transit connects directly to the refuge entrance.

Safety: Watch for incoming tides on Plum Island beaches. Carry cash for refuge entry if not using annual pass.

Crowd patterns: Fewer visitors after Labor Day means shorter waits at restaurants and easier parking at the refuge.

Local etiquette: Yield to pedestrians on the boardwalk. Keep dogs leashed inside the wildlife refuge.
