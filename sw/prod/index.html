What to Know About St. George, Utah

St. George in October offers a pleasant climate for exploring outdoor attractions. Days are warm and nights cool, perfect for hiking and sightseeing.

Local customs: Politeness is valued; greet locals with a friendly hello. Tipping in restaurants is customary.

Best times of day: Early mornings are ideal for cooler temperatures, while evenings offer beautiful sunsets over the red rocks.

Transportation quirks: Most attractions are accessible by car; public transportation options are limited.

Safety: Stay hydrated while hiking, as desert conditions can be dry. Keep an eye out for wildlife on trails.

Crowd patterns: Expect fewer visitors in October compared to summer months, making it easier to enjoy popular sites.

Local etiquette: Dress modestly when visiting local churches or historical sites. Respect the environment by staying on designated trails.

NPS / Ally O'Rullian

What to Know About Zion National Park

Zion National Park in October presents mild temperatures and beautiful fall foliage. Expect crowds at popular attractions, especially on weekends. Prepare for varying conditions in the canyon, including possible water in The Narrows.

Local customs: Respect the natural environment and adhere to park regulations. Stay on designated trails to protect the ecosystem.

Best times of day: Early morning is ideal for hikes to avoid crowds and to see wildlife. Late afternoon offers great lighting for photography.

Transportation quirks: Park shuttles are mandatory for accessing Zion Canyon. No private vehicles are allowed on the scenic drive during peak season.

Safety: Stay hydrated and be mindful of changing weather conditions in the canyon. Watch for flash floods in slot canyons.

Crowd patterns: Expect busy conditions, particularly on weekends and holidays. Arrive early to secure parking and enjoy popular hikes.

Local etiquette: Be courteous to fellow hikers and maintain noise levels. Pack out what you pack in to keep the park clean.

NPS / Ally O'Rullian

Typical October temperatures are around 62°F daytime and 38°F overnight. Fall colors transform the landscape, with cottonwood and aspen turning vibrant gold. Expect moderate crowds as this is a popular shoulder season. The park shuttle is required in Zion Canyon; no private vehicles allowed.
