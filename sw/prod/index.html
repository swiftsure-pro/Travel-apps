What to Know About St. George, Utah

St. George in October features pleasant weather for outdoor activities, with daytime highs around 72°F and cooler nights. It's a great time to explore the area's unique geology and historical sites.

Local customs: Residents are friendly and welcoming. Tipping around 15-20% is standard in restaurants.

Best times of day: Mornings are ideal for hiking due to cooler temperatures. Evenings are perfect for dining and enjoying the sunset.

Transportation quirks: Most attractions are accessible by car, and parking is readily available. Public transport options are limited.

Safety: Stay hydrated during hikes and be cautious of changing weather conditions, especially in the desert.

Crowd patterns: Expect lighter crowds in October compared to the summer months, making it easier to explore popular sites.

Local etiquette: Respect local wildlife and the natural environment. Keep noise levels down in residential areas.

What to Know About Zion National Park

Zion National Park in October is a great time to visit, with pleasant temperatures and beautiful fall colors. Prepare for potential crowds and ensure to arrive early for shuttle access. Be mindful of trail conditions and water temperatures if hiking The Narrows.

Local customs: Respect the natural environment and follow Leave No Trace principles. Keep noise levels low to preserve the natural experience for others.

Best times of day: Early morning is ideal for hiking to avoid crowds and enjoy cooler temperatures. Late afternoon provides optimal lighting for photography.

Transportation quirks: The park operates a shuttle system that is mandatory for accessing Zion Canyon during peak season. Be sure to check the shuttle schedule upon arrival.

Safety: Stay hydrated and be aware of your surroundings, especially on steep trails. Carry a map and have a plan for hikes, particularly in less-traveled areas.

Crowd patterns: Expect more visitors on weekends and during school holidays. Early in the morning or later in the afternoon can help avoid peak crowds.

Local etiquette: Maintain a respectful distance from wildlife and do not feed animals. Follow marked trails to protect the park's natural resources.

Crowd patterns: Crowds are lighter in October compared to summer, especially on weekdays. Popular viewpoints may still see visitors during peak hours.

Local etiquette: Follow Leave No Trace principles. Pack out any trash and stay on designated trails.
