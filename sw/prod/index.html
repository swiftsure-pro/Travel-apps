What to Know About St. George, Utah

St. George in October features mild temperatures and fewer crowds, making it ideal for outdoor activities. Expect highs around 72°F and lows around 38°F.

Local customs: Residents are friendly and often engage in conversations with visitors. Tipping is customary at restaurants, typically 15-20%.

Best times of day: Mornings are cooler, making them perfect for hikes. Late afternoons are great for exploring local attractions before dinner.

Transportation quirks: Public transportation is limited; a car is recommended for getting around. Parking is generally available at attractions.

Safety: Stay hydrated and wear sunscreen, especially during outdoor activities. Be mindful of wildlife in desert areas.

Crowd patterns: October sees fewer tourists compared to summer months, resulting in less crowded attractions and trails.

Local etiquette: Respect local customs and the natural environment. Clean up after yourself and follow trail guidelines.

What to Know About Zion National Park

Zion National Park in October features mild weather and fewer crowds, making it an excellent time to explore. Be aware of shuttle requirements for canyon access and prepare for variable conditions in the water for river hikes.

Local customs: Respect the natural environment and follow Leave No Trace principles. Many visitors enjoy sharing trail experiences, so be friendly but respectful.

Best times of day: Mornings are ideal for cooler temperatures and quieter trails, especially for popular hikes. Late afternoons offer great lighting for photography.

Transportation quirks: The park shuttle is mandatory for accessing the main canyon; personal vehicles are not allowed during peak hours. Parking is limited, so arrive early.

Safety: Stay hydrated and wear appropriate footwear, especially for water hikes. Be cautious of slippery rocks in The Narrows.

Crowd patterns: Expect the park to be less crowded in October compared to summer. Weekdays tend to be quieter than weekends.

Local etiquette: Stay on marked trails and respect wildlife. Keep noise levels down to preserve the tranquility of the park.

NPS / Ally O'Rullian
