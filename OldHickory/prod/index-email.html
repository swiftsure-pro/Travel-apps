Transportation quirks: Public transport options are limited; a car is recommended for getting around effectively.

Safety: Stay aware of your surroundings, especially in less populated areas. Keep personal items secure.

Crowd patterns: Expect lighter crowds at major attractions compared to summer months. Weekends may see more visitors.

Local etiquette: It's polite to say 'thank you' and 'please' when interacting with service staff. Tipping 15-20% is customary.
