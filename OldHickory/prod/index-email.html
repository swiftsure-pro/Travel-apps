What to Know About Old Hickory, Tennessee

Old Hickory in December offers mild weather and fewer crowds. It’s a great time to explore historical sites and enjoy the tranquility of nature. Be prepared for potential rain and pack accordingly.

Local customs: Tennessee is known for its hospitality. It's common to greet locals with a friendly smile or 'hello'.

Best times of day: Mornings are often quiet and great for exploring outdoor attractions. Late afternoons offer beautiful light for photography.

Transportation quirks: Public transportation options are limited, so a car is recommended for getting around. Rideshare services are widely available.

Safety: Stay aware of your surroundings, especially in busy areas. The winter weather can bring slippery conditions, so drive cautiously.

Crowd patterns: December sees fewer tourists compared to summer months. Attractions are less crowded, making it easier to explore.

Local etiquette: Tip service workers around 15-20%. Always say 'thank you' when receiving assistance.
