Transportation quirks: Public transport options are limited, so having a car is essential for getting around Old Hickory and nearby areas.

Safety: Stay aware of your surroundings, especially near the lake. Always follow local guidelines for outdoor activities.

Crowd patterns: Expect fewer crowds in December, particularly at popular attractions like Andrew Jackson's Hermitage. Weekdays are quieter than weekends.
