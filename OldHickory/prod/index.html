What to Know About Old Hickory, Tennessee

Old Hickory in December is a peaceful winter destination with a focus on outdoor activities and local history. Visitors can enjoy attractions with fewer crowds and explore the beautiful scenery around Old Hickory Lake.

Local customs: Tennessee locals are friendly and welcoming. It's common to greet strangers with a smile or nod.

Best times of day: Early mornings and late afternoons are ideal for outdoor activities. Enjoying the sunset by the lake can be particularly rewarding.

Transportation quirks: Local public transportation options are limited, so a car is recommended for getting around. Rideshare services are available in the area.

Safety: Watch for icy patches on paths and roads during winter months. Stay hydrated, especially if engaging in outdoor activities.

Crowd patterns: December sees fewer tourists compared to the summer months, allowing for a more relaxed experience at popular attractions.

Local etiquette: It's polite to say 'thank you' and 'please' in interactions. Tipping 15-20% is customary in restaurants.

What to Know About Asheville, North Carolina

Asheville offers a festive winter atmosphere with plenty to explore during your December visit. Expect mild daytime temperatures, but prepare for colder nights. The Biltmore Estate is a highlight, with special holiday events. Parking can be challenging downtown, so plan accordingly.

Local customs: Asheville residents appreciate local art and culture; supporting local businesses is encouraged.

Best times of day: Mornings are ideal for visiting popular attractions before crowds arrive. Evenings are perfect for enjoying the holiday lights in downtown.

Transportation quirks: Public transportation is available, but many visitors choose to drive for convenience. Rideshare services operate throughout the city.

Safety: Stay aware of icy conditions on sidewalks and trails. Always lock your car and keep valuables out of sight.

Crowd patterns: Weekends can be busy, especially at the Biltmore Estate and downtown. Arriving early helps avoid long lines.

Local etiquette: Be respectful in galleries and studios by not touching artwork unless invited. Tipping 15-20% is customary in restaurants.
