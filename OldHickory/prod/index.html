What to Know About Old Hickory, Tennessee

Old Hickory sits 15 miles east of Nashville International Airport with lodging check-in at 4 PM on December 6. The 7-day stay centers on winter visits to the Hermitage and lake access before the drive to Asheville.

Local customs: Many restaurants seat parties at shared tables during peak hours; confirm seating preference on arrival.

Best times of day: Morning light reaches the Hermitage grounds first; lake access remains quiet until mid-morning.

Transportation quirks: I-40 eastbound from the airport carries heavy afternoon commuter traffic; allow 35 minutes instead of the posted 25.

Safety: December nights drop near freezing; carry a flashlight on unlit lake trails after 5 PM.

Crowd patterns: Holiday visitors increase at the Hermitage between December 10-13; arrive before 10 AM for shorter lines.

Local etiquette: Tipping 20 percent remains standard at independent restaurants even for counter service.
