Transportation quirks: TN-45 provides the main east-west access; watch for freight trains crossing at grade near the dam.

Crowd patterns: December sees minimal visitors at the Hermitage compared with spring and fall; parking remains open at all hours.

Local etiquette: Fishing from public piers requires a Tennessee license; check limits posted at each ramp.
