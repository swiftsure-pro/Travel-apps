What to Know About Old Hickory, Tennessee

Old Hickory sits on the Cumberland River 15 miles northeast of downtown Nashville. December weather stays cool with possible rain; most attractions operate on winter schedules.

Local customs: Many restaurants close by 9 PM on weekdays.

Best times of day: Morning light on the lake improves visibility for photography before 10 AM.

Transportation quirks: Traffic on I-40 eastbound increases after 4 PM on weekdays.

Safety: Watch for slippery surfaces near the dam spillway after rain.

Crowd patterns: Visitor numbers remain low through the first two weeks of December.

Local etiquette: Wait to be seated at family-style restaurants even when tables appear open.
