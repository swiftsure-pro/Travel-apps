What to Know About Old Hickory, Tennessee

Old Hickory sits on the Cumberland River 15 miles northeast of Nashville. December weather brings occasional light rain and temperatures that drop below freezing at night. The five-day stay allows time for the Hermitage and lake access before the drive to Asheville.

Local customs: Tennessee requires a fishing license for anyone 13 and older on Old Hickory Lake; daily permits are sold at local bait shops.

Best times of day: Morning light on the lake is clearest before 10 AM when winds are calm. The Hermitage grounds receive steady foot traffic after 11 AM.

Transportation quirks: Old Hickory Boulevard carries steady commuter traffic during weekday rush hours; allow extra time for short drives.

Safety: Watch for black ice on shaded road sections near the lake after overnight freezes.

Crowd patterns: Visitor numbers at the Hermitage stay low through mid-December until holiday events begin closer to Christmas.

Local etiquette: Boaters on Old Hickory Lake keep wakes low near shoreline homes; maintain 200 feet from docks when possible.
